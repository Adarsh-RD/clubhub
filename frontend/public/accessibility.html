Club Hub

Club Hub is a place where every member of the campus can find opportunity

Whatever your goals, ideas, and abilities are, we're here to help you succeed.

No two Club Hub members are the same—and it's that diversity which makes our student community so great.

We're on a journey to make accessibility and inclusive design part of our core principles, building accessibility from the ground up and testing our platform with assistive technology to make sure that everyone can use Club Hub to advance their campus goals.

Our Accessibility Answer Desk

Our teams are constantly working to improve the experience for all of Club Hub's features by adding new improvements and making changes to better serve all our students and faculty. We're always open to feedback and would love to hear from you as to how we can make our platform even better.

If you find an accessibility bug, want to submit a complaint, or have trouble using Club Hub's platform with assistive technology, contact us and we'll reach out to help.
