Your Privacy Matters

Club Hub's mission is to connect students and clubs across campus to allow them to be more productive, engaged, and successful. Central to this mission is our commitment to be transparent about the data we collect about you, how it is used, and with whom it is shared.

This Privacy Policy applies when you use our Services (described below). We offer our users choices about the data we collect, use and share as described in this Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Settings and our Help Center.

Key Terms

1. Data We Collect

Registration: To create an account you need to provide data including your name, email address and/or mobile number, and a password. If you register for a premium service or as a club coordinator, you will need to provide payment (e.g., credit card) and billing information.

Profile: You have choices about the information on your profile, such as your education, club affiliations, skills, photo, city or area and endorsements. You don't have to provide additional information on your profile; however, profile information helps you to get more from our Services, including finding communities and campus events.
